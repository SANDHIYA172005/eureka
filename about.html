Our story

Shaping futures with purpose.

Since 2014, Eureka Academy & S3 Dance Studio has been a place in Moolapalayam, Erode where children grow into confident, skilled and independent individuals.

We bring together education, arts, dance, skills and fitness — from Abacus, Phonics and Handwriting to Western Dance and Zumba — with every session available online and offline.
